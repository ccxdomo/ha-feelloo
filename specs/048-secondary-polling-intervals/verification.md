## 3. Harness history (048 session)

The first run of the extended harness produced 370/379. The 9 failures were a **harness bug, not a product bug**: the H6/H7 entity collectors were written as `async def` coroutines, but HA's `AddEntitiesCallback` is invoked synchronously (`async_add_entities(entities)` is not awaited — HA schedules the platform's internal coroutine itself), so the coroutines were never awaited and the platforms' output was never collected (the "SET identical" checks passed vacuously on empty collections — which the paired "== enumerated surface" checks immediately exposed). The collectors are now plain sync functions, the faithful miniature of `AddEntitiesCallback`; all 379 checks pass. Recorded for the same honesty reason as the 047 harness-history note: a harness that once failed must say so.
